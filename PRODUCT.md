# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

(Electron desktop app for macOS and Windows; the UI is a web renderer, with native window chrome per OS.)

## Users
- Primary: the shop owner or attendant of a small Brazilian retail business (first customer: an optical store / jewelry, "Equipe da Visão"). Not technical. Uses the app on the store's computer between customers to answer WhatsApp messages, send promotions to past customers, and manage WhatsApp groups.
- The agency (Danilo's) installs it and supports the client, and will sell/license it as its own product to other small businesses.

## Product Purpose
Let a small business run its WhatsApp relationship from one desktop app: conversations, campaigns to its customer base, group management, automatic replies and recurring reminders, without getting the number banned and without losing data. Success = the shop sends its monthly promotions and answers customers safely, with zero lost sessions or data.

## Positioning
Local-first: data, session and backups stay on the shop's computer (backup to a folder the user chooses, encrypted). Anti-ban protection is built in by default, not an expert setting. Every module can be switched on/off per account, so a simple shop sees a simple app.

## Operating Context
- Shop counter computer, often a modest Windows PC; also Macs at the agency.
- Customer lists arrive as Excel spreadsheets (name, phone, sometimes birthday/purchase date).
- Campaigns tied to retail dates (Black Friday, Mother's Day, etc.), image + caption.
- One or more WhatsApp numbers per business; the agency may run several businesses on one install.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Modules: Accounts, Conversations (send/receive), Contacts, Campaigns, Anti-ban, Groups (send, extract members, create, moderation; each toggleable), Auto-replies, Recurring reminders, Backup, Settings.
- Unofficial WhatsApp client (Baileys): ban risk is real; UI must communicate limits honestly.
- Brazilian Portuguese only. LGPD: opt-out list, data export/deletion, masked phones in logs.
- Product name: undecided (working name "Disparador"). Do not invent a brand name.

## Brand Commitments
None yet. Must not use WhatsApp's name or logo as the product brand.

## Evidence on Hand
No testimonials, metrics or customer logos exist. Do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles
1. Simple for a non-technical shop owner first; power features hide behind module toggles.
2. Safe by default: protection against bans and data loss is on without configuration.
3. Honest status: always show clearly whether WhatsApp is connected, sending, paused, or at risk.
4. Local and private: the shop's customer data never leaves its computer unless it chooses a backup folder.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Users of varied age and digital literacy: large readable text, clear plain-language labels, high contrast, full keyboard use.
